Accessibility

We want this website to be usable by everyone.

What we do

  • All pages can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to content” link.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast guidelines.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative drawings are hidden from screen readers.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, animations and looping videos are switched off.
  • Pages scale to small screens and to 200% zoom without loss of content.

Known limitations

The interactive ratchet simulation on the research page is a visual demonstration; its text explains the same idea in words. The publication and news lists need JavaScript.

Feedback

If something does not work for you, please tell us at emanuele.penocchio@uni-mainz.de and we will fix it.
