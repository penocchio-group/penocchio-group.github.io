Join us

We are building the team now. If the questions on our research page make you curious, we would love to hear from you, whether you are a chemist, a physicist or a mathematician at heart.

Email Emanuele Read about our research

Updated October 2026. We are recruiting PhD students and postdoctoral researchers for 2026–2027, funded by the Wübben Stiftung Wissenschaft and Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz. We review applications as they arrive until positions are filled. If none of the roles below fits exactly, write anyway.

PhD student

  • Dr. rer. nat. from JGU
  • Usually 3 years
  • Salary: TV-L E13 scale
  • Start date flexible

Work on one of our three questions: functional reaction networks, molecular machines by design, or programmable electrochemistry. You will develop theory or code, work with experimental partners and present at international conferences.

  • Master’s degree in chemistry, physics, applied mathematics or a related subject
  • Enjoy maths, coding, or both; no prior experience with nonequilibrium physics needed
  • PhD students can also join structured graduate programmes in Mainz, such as the Max Planck Graduate Center

Postdoctoral researcher

  • Up to 3 years
  • Salary: TV-L E13 scale
  • Simulation or electrochemistry

Help build our simulation platform for molecular machines, or our reaction–diffusion models for waveform-driven electrochemistry. Postdocs co-mentor students and are encouraged to develop their own research line.

  • PhD in theoretical or computational chemistry, physics or a related field
  • Experience with molecular dynamics, code development, or modelling with differential equations

Bachelor’s and Master’s theses

  • JGU and visiting students
  • Weeks to a full thesis

Self-contained projects in theory or simulation, from a few weeks to a full Master’s thesis. A good way to find out whether theory is for you.

  • Chemistry, physics, biomedical chemistry or mathematics students
  • Send a short email with your interests

Bring your own fellowship

  • MSCA
  • Humboldt
  • DAAD
  • and others

We gladly host and support applications for independent fellowships such as Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships, Humboldt Research Fellowships or DAAD scholarships. Emanuele won a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowship himself and will help you with yours.

  • Contact us at least two months before the deadline

How to apply

One email is enough to start. Please put the position in the subject line, for example “PhD application” or “Master’s thesis”.

  1. Your CVEducation, research experience and any publications or code.
  2. A short letterOne page on what interests you in our research and why.
  3. TranscriptsGrades from your Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees.
  4. Two refereesNames and email addresses; we contact them only if you are shortlisted.

Shortlisted candidates are invited to a video call and a short presentation. Send your application to emanuele.penocchio@uni-mainz.de.

Questions people ask

I am a chemist and have never done theory. Can I still apply?
Yes. Emanuele studied chemistry before his PhD in physics. What matters is curiosity and a willingness to learn the maths and coding your project needs.
I am a physicist or mathematician. Is a chemistry department right for me?
Yes. Our questions sit between chemistry and physics, and the theory we use is statistical mechanics, stochastic processes and dynamical systems. A physics or applied-mathematics Master’s qualifies you for a PhD in our group; you will pick up the chemistry along the way, with help from our experimental collaborators.
Do I need to speak German?
No. The group works in English.
How much programming do I need?
Some experience with Python, Julia, C or similar helps, but it is not a requirement for PhD students. We use version control and share code within the group, so you will learn good habits along the way.
Is there any lab work?
Most projects are theory and simulation. Projects on electrochemistry can include experiments, and everyone works closely with experimental collaborators.
Will I go to conferences?
Yes. We budget for travel so that every member presents at international meetings, and we plan workshops in Mainz.
