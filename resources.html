Reading list

Seven papers to get into molecular ratchets and nonequilibrium thermodynamics, roughly from easiest to hardest. Ideal before a thesis or PhD interview.

  • Start here
    Artificial molecular ratchets: tools enabling endergonic processes
    T. Sangchai, S. Al Shehimy, E. Penocchio, G. Ragazzon · Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 62, e202309501 (2023)
    Our tutorial review: what a molecular ratchet is and how to analyse one.
  • Start here
    Kinetic barrier diagrams to visualize and engineer molecular nonequilibrium systems
    E. Penocchio, G. Ragazzon · Small 19, 2206188 (2023)
    A pictorial way to reason about ratchets without solving equations.
  • Classic
    Thermodynamics and kinetics of a Brownian motor
    R. D. Astumian · Science 276, 917–922 (1997)
    The flashing ratchet from our research page, explained by one of the founders of the field.
  • Classic
    Synthetic molecular motors and mechanical machines
    E. R. Kay, D. A. Leigh, F. Zerbetto · Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 46, 72–191 (2007)
    The comprehensive review that introduced a generation of chemists to molecular machines.
  • Theory
    Nonequilibrium thermodynamics of chemical reaction networks: wisdom from stochastic thermodynamics
    R. Rao, M. Esposito · Phys. Rev. X 6, 041064 (2016)
    The framework behind much of our work on energy flows in chemistry.
  • Theory
    Thermodynamic efficiency in dissipative chemistry
    E. Penocchio, R. Rao, M. Esposito · Nat. Commun. 10, 3865 (2019)
    How to define and measure the efficiency of chemically fuelled processes.
  • Going deeper
    Methods and conversations in (post)modern thermodynamics
    F. Avanzini et al. · SciPost Phys. Lect. Notes 80 (2024)
    Free lecture notes from a doctoral school on modern nonequilibrium thermodynamics.
