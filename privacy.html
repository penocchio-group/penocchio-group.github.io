Privacy

Short version: this website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from third-party servers.

Controller

Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz, Saarstraße 21, 55122 Mainz, Germany. Contact for this website: Jun.-Prof. Dr. Emanuele Penocchio, emanuele.penocchio@uni-mainz.de. The university’s data protection officer can be reached via the JGU website.

What happens when you visit

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and embeds no content from other servers: fonts, images and videos are all served from the same host.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub records your IP address in its server logs to keep the site and its service secure, whether or not you have a GitHub account (GitHub’s description). We have no access to these logs. Because GitHub is based in the United States, the data may be processed there; GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. How long GitHub keeps the data, and your rights towards GitHub, are set out in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Lab area

The members’ area is decrypted in your browser. The password is never sent to any server.

Email

If you write to us, we use your message and contact details only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed. Application documents are handled according to JGU’s rules for recruitment.

External links

Links to other websites (journals, Google Scholar, ORCID, GitHub) are ordinary links. Those sites’ own privacy policies apply once you follow them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Rhineland-Palatinate: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz).
